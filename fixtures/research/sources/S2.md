# Reduced working time and software delivery: evidence from engineering telemetry

- id: S2
- author: Okafor, N. and Lindqvist, M.
- org: KTH Royal Institute of Technology
- date: 2025-11-02
- type: peer-reviewed
- url: https://example.org/research/s2-engineering-telemetry
- basis: 14 software companies, 410 engineers, difference-in-differences on merged PRs and deploys

Merged pull requests per engineer per week did not change significantly after the switch (−2%, CI −7% to +3%). Meeting hours dropped by 30%. Teams with on-call rotations saw median incident acknowledgement time rise.

## Reported findings
- (output) Objective delivery metrics (PRs, deploys) unchanged. [PRs/engineer −2% (CI −7%..+3%)]
- (collaboration) Fewer meetings, but cross-timezone handoffs got harder. [meeting hours −30%]
- (on-call) Median incident acknowledgement time increased for on-call teams. [median ack +11 min]
