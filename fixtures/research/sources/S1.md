# Four-day week pilot: 12-month outcomes across 61 organisations

- id: S1
- author: Schor, J. et al.
- org: Boston College / 4 Day Week Global
- date: 2025-03-14
- type: peer-reviewed
- url: https://example.org/research/s1-four-day-week-pilot
- basis: 61 organisations, ~2,900 employees, 12 months, self-selected participants

Burnout scores fell significantly and stayed lower at 12 months. Self-reported productivity was maintained; organisational revenue was flat to slightly up. Participating firms opted in, which limits generalisation.

## Reported findings
- (wellbeing) Burnout fell and stayed lower at 12 months. [71% reported lower burnout]
- (output) Productivity was maintained, not increased, by self-report and revenue.
- (retention) Resignations declined during the trial. [resignations -57%]
