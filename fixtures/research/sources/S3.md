# Shorter working week trial: final evaluation report

- id: S3
- author: Department for Work Evaluation Unit
- org: UK Government (illustrative)
- date: 2024-09-20
- type: government
- url: https://example.org/research/s3-government-evaluation
- basis: Public-sector IT and admin teams, 18 months, matched comparison group

Staff wellbeing and retention improved relative to comparison teams. Service performance indicators were maintained. Out-of-hours IT support required additional rota planning.

## Reported findings
- (wellbeing) Wellbeing improved versus a matched comparison group.
- (retention) Staff turnover fell versus the comparison group. [turnover −35%]
- (output) Service performance indicators were maintained.
- (on-call) Out-of-hours support needed extra rota planning; no SLA breach reported.
