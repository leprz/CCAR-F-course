# Hours reduction and output in knowledge work: a randomised field experiment

- id: S9
- author: Tanaka, H. and Moretti, E.
- org: NBER working paper
- date: 2026-04-30
- type: peer-reviewed
- url: https://example.org/research/s9-field-experiment
- basis: Randomised: 1,100 employees at a software services firm, 9 months

Treated teams delivered 9% fewer client deliverables per week; output per hour rose 13%. Retention improved. Effects on output were driven by client-facing teams with fixed response obligations.

## Reported findings
- (output) Total weekly deliverables fell 9%, though output per hour rose 13%. [deliverables −9%, per-hour +13%]
- (retention) Retention improved in treated teams.
