# State of Developer Work 2026

- id: S4
- author: Survey team
- org: DevPulse (developer survey)
- date: 2026-02-10
- type: survey
- url: https://example.org/research/s4-devpulse-survey
- basis: 8,200 self-selected respondents; 6% work a four-day week

Developers on four-day weeks report higher job satisfaction and say they would not return to five days. They also report more context switching on their working days.

## Reported findings
- (wellbeing) Higher reported satisfaction among four-day-week developers.
- (collaboration) More reported context switching on working days.
